These terms govern your use of LawGovern. By creating an account you accept them. If you are accepting on behalf of a firm, you confirm you may bind it.

1. What LawGovern is

A working tool for Company Secretaries and compliance teams. It keeps a register of what applies to each entity you enter, computes statutory deadlines where a provision states one, records the evidence that a filing was made, runs statutory calculations, and reports where those things disagree with one another.

2. What LawGovern is not

It is not legal or professional advice

Nothing the product outputs is advice. You are the professional. You sign the filing, you carry the responsibility for it, and nothing here displaces your own judgement or your obligations to your client and to your institute.

Nothing is verified against MCA21 or the exchanges

The product has no connection to MCA21, to the stock exchanges, or to any other official filing system. It cannot confirm that a filing was actually made.

When a screen shows a filing as recorded, that means somebody typed it in. Where the register says nothing is recorded against an obligation, that is a statement about the register and not an assertion that the filing was missed. Verify on the MCA or SEBI portal before you rely on any of it.

The rule corpus is not guaranteed to be current

The obligations, deadlines and thresholds in the product were built from spreadsheets and from published texts held within it. As at the date above:

  • the SEBI LODR text held is amended to 14 July 2026;
  • the SEBI PIT text held is amended to 12 March 2025;
  • the Companies Act, 2013 text held is amended only to 1 April 2021, which is over five years old.

Every rule carries a verification status you can read at Administration → Rule Governance, and a rule nobody has checked says “never checked” rather than pretending otherwise. Check the provision before you act on a figure or a date.

It is also not a filing service, not a registered office service, and not a substitute for professional indemnity insurance.
